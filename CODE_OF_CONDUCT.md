# Code of Conduct

MeshTransit follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback
and focus on what is best for the community.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through a GitHub
security advisory marked "Code of Conduct" or by contacting a maintainer
directly. All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially. Maintainers may
remove, edit or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors who violate this code.
